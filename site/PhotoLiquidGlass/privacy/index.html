隐私政策

Photo Liquid Glass 尊重并保护你的照片与个人信息。应用的照片处理在设备本地完成。

我们不收集的数据

我们不收集、上传、出售或共享你的照片、实况照片、拍摄参数、设备型号、拍摄时间、位置信息或其他照片元数据。应用不包含广告、行为分析或用户跟踪 SDK，也不要求创建账户。

照片访问与本地处理

只有在你主动选择照片或保存处理结果时，应用才会请求系统照片权限。水印渲染、元数据读取及导出均在本机完成。生成结果由你决定是否保存至系统照片图库。

iCloud 照片

如果你选择的照片尚未下载到设备，Apple 的系统照片框架可能从 iCloud 获取对应资源。该传输由 Apple 的系统服务处理，应用不会把照片发送到开发者服务器。

App 内购买

自愿支持项目由 Apple StoreKit 与 App Store 处理。应用可获得商品标识和交易验证结果，但不会获得或保存你的付款卡号、账单资料或 Apple Account 密码。购买不会解锁额外功能。

数据保留与删除

我们不运营用于保存用户数据的服务器。处理过程中产生的临时文件会在任务完成后移除。保存到照片图库的结果由你通过系统照片 App 管理和删除。

第三方服务

应用使用 Apple 提供的系统框架，包括 Photos、iCloud Photos、StoreKit、App Intents 与 Shortcuts。相关系统服务受 Apple 自己的条款与隐私政策约束。

第三方服務

App 使用 Apple 提供的系統框架，包括 Photos、iCloud Photos、StoreKit、App Intents 與 Shortcuts。相關系統服務受 Apple 自己的條款與隱私權政策約束。

Privacy Policy

Photo Liquid Glass respects and protects your photos and personal information. Photo processing takes place locally on your device.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, upload, sell, or share your photos, Live Photos, camera settings, device model, capture time, location information, or other photo metadata. The app contains no advertising, behavioral analytics, or user-tracking SDKs and does not require an account.

Photo Access and Local Processing

The app requests system photo permissions only when you choose a photo or save a processed result. Watermark rendering, metadata reading, and export occur locally. You decide whether to save generated results to your system Photo Library.

iCloud Photos

If a selected photo has not been downloaded to your device, Apple's system photo framework may retrieve the corresponding resource from iCloud. Apple system services handle this transfer. The app does not send photos to a developer-operated server.

In-App Purchases

Optional tips are processed by Apple StoreKit and the App Store. The app may receive a product identifier and transaction verification result, but it does not receive or store your payment card number, billing details, or Apple Account password. Purchases do not unlock additional features.

Data Retention and Deletion

We do not operate a server that stores user data. Temporary files created during processing are removed after the task completes. You manage and delete results saved to your Photo Library through the system Photos app.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Apple-provided system frameworks, including Photos, iCloud Photos, StoreKit, App Intents, and Shortcuts. These system services are governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

For privacy questions, email banana@hxhlb.top or visit the support page.
